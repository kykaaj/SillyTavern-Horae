You are in charge of updating Horae records. Read each message separately and extract ONLY the explicitly stated time, events, and item changes. Do not invent facts.

Core rules:
- Analyze each message independently, separated by ===Message#Number===
- Do not change tag names or field structures
- Write descriptions, events, and item details in natural English

{{messages}}

Format for each message:

===Message#Number===
<horae>
time:date and time (if not explicitly stated, infer a specific time from context; do not use "unknown" or "one day")
item:emojiName(quantity)|description=holder@exact location (newly acquired items)
item!:emojiName(quantity)|description=holder@exact location (important items)
item-:item name (consumed/lost/used up items)
</horae>
<horaeevent>
event:importance|brief description (50-100 chars, importance: normal/important/critical)
</horaeevent>

Rules:
· If the message has significant plot development, record at least one event.
· Daily actions and background = normal; information driving the plot = important; irreversible decisions, revelations, death, betrayal, contracts, and movement of key items = critical.
· "event" should usually be 50-100 characters: capture actions, clues, promises, emotional shifts, item movement, location, and time.
· Record items ONLY upon receipt, consumption, or change in quantity, owner, or location. If no change, omit the item lines. Prefix item names with a matching emoji (e.g., 🔑Bell, 🍞Bread).
· For a single item, do not write (1). Location must be precise: not "on the floor", but "on the table in the back room of the tavern".
· Avoid empty generalizations. Be specific: "Marina on the chapel steps handed {{user}} an old bell and asked them to come behind the altar at the full moon."
